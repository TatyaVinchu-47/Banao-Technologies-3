---

## What We Propose

**Phase 1: Nesting Performance Dashboard** — an 8-week project to give you clear visibility into material utilisation.

We will:
- Connect to your existing Strumis data (no disruption to current operations)
- Build a dashboard showing utilisation rates by project, material type, and time period
- Track offcut inventory and reuse rates
- Enable attribution of losses to client revisions vs. internal factors

**What this gives you:**
- Weekly visibility into wastage across all projects
- Data to support client revision claims
- Evidence base to identify where improvements will have the most impact
- Foundation for future optimisation (Phase 2)
